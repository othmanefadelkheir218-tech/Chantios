==============================================================================
 1. THE BIG PICTURE
==============================================================================

 ChantierOS is a SaaS (software you rent every month) for renovation and
 construction companies, mainly in Belgium (VAT 6% / 21%, languages FR / NL /
 EN).

 Each company that uses it is called a "TENANT". Every company sees only its
 own data. No company can ever see another company's data.

 The app helps a small construction company answer these questions:

   - Who are my clients and what did I promise them?      (clients, quotes)
   - What work is planned, and who does it?               (projects, planning)
   - How many hours did people work?                      (time entries)
   - What materials do I have, and what did each job use? (stock)
   - What did my subcontractors and suppliers bill me?    (purchase invoices)
   - Am I making or losing money on each job?             (margin)
   - Who owes me money, and is it late?                   (invoices, payments)
   - Can my client see where we are, without calling me?  (client portal)

 THE MAIN LOOP (one job):

   client -> project -> quote -> accepted -> planning -> work on site
          (materials + subcontractors + hours) -> real cost vs budget (margin)
          -> invoices -> payment -> client sees everything in the portal

 Every feature feeds the next one. For example the margin is only correct
 because quotes, stock, subcontractor bills and hours all give it real
 numbers.

 THE 4 GOLDEN RULES OF THE PRODUCT

   1. Nothing is "typed twice". A number is stored in ONE place and read
      everywhere else (budget, stock quantity, progress %, margin).
   2. History is never rewritten. Stock, payments, prices and hourly rates
      are "frozen" when they happen. A correction is a NEW line, not an edit.
   3. Costs are counted ONCE (a tile is counted when it leaves the stock, not
      again when the supplier bill is entered).
   4. The client sees only what the company decides to show. Costs, margin,
      subcontractors, employees' names and hours are never visible to them.

 VOCABULARY

   Tenant ........ a company that uses the app
   Platform admin  ChantierOS staff (the owner of the product)
   Quote ......... the price offer sent to a client (French: "devis")
   Invoice ....... the bill the company sends to a client (money IN)
   Purchase invoice  a bill the company RECEIVES from a supplier or
                  subcontractor (money OUT)
   Budget ........ the total of the ACCEPTED quotes of a project
   Margin ........ what the company keeps = budget - all real costs
   Ledger ........ a list of events that is only added to, never edited
   Frozen ........ copied at the moment of the event, so it never changes
   Draft/Sent .... a document being prepared / a document already sent

 2. THE TWO WORLDS: THE PLATFORM AND THE COMPANIES
==============================================================================

 There are two completely separate groups of users. They never mix.

 A) PLATFORM SIDE ("admin_users") - the staff of ChantierOS
      - See and manage ALL companies.
      - Two kinds: "super_admin" and "staff".
      - Login with email + password + a 2FA code (authenticator app).
      - They create plans, suspend companies, answer support tickets.

 B) COMPANY SIDE ("users") - everybody inside one company
      - Belong to ONE company only.
      - See only the data of that company.
      - 7 fixed roles (see section 3).

 C) CLIENTS (the customers of a company) are NOT users. They have no account
    and no password. They get a private link (the "portal") and/or emails.

 One email address can belong to ONE company only. (Login has no "company"
 field, so the same email cannot exist in two companies.)

 3. PEOPLE AND ROLES (WHO CAN DO WHAT)
==============================================================================

 The 7 company roles are fixed:

   admin ............ "Main Manager" - the boss. Can do everything, and is the
                      only one who manages the team, settings and billing.
   manager .......... "Sub Manager" - runs projects day to day.
   site_supervisor .. runs the site: reports, material usage, hours.
   team_leader ...... leads a team on site: tasks, hours, reports.
   worker ........... the field person. PHONE ONLY: my tasks + log my hours.
   sales ............ clients and quotes.
   accountant ....... invoices, purchase bills, margins.

 DEFAULT PERMISSIONS (per module)

   full = see + create + edit + delete.   view = read only.
   own  = only the user's own rows.       -    = no access.

   Module              admin  manager  site_sup  team_lead  worker  sales  accountant
   ------------------  -----  -------  --------  ---------  ------  -----  ----------
   clients             full   full     view      -          -       full   view
   projects            full   full     full      view       -       view   view
   tasks               full   full     full      full       own(v)  -      -
   time_entries        full   full     full      full       own     -      -
   catalogue           full   full     view      -          -       view   -
   stock               full   full     view      view       -       -      view
   quotes              full   full     -         -          -       full   view
   invoices            full   -        -         -          -       view   full
   purchase_invoices   full   -        -         -          -       -      full
   margins             full   full     -         -          -       -      full
   subcontractors      full   full     view      -          -       -      view
   reports             full   full     full      full       own(v)  -      -
   media (files)       full   full     full      full       own     view   view
   chat                full   full     full      full       own     full   full
   team                full   view     -         -          -       -      -
   settings            full   -        -         -          -       -      -
   analytics           view   -        -         -          -       -      view
   contacts            view   view     -         -          -       -      -
   notes               own    own      own       own        own     own    own
   email               full   full     -         -          never   full   -

 THE COMPANY BOSS CAN CHANGE THESE PER ROLE
   The table above is the DEFAULT. The admin of a company can override it for
   his own company only (example: let the manager see invoices). Only the
   differences are stored.

 THE "OWN" LEVEL
   "own" means "yes, but only my rows". A worker sees only HIS tasks and logs
   only HIS hours. A worker can never see the team's rows.

 WHO MANAGES THE TEAM
   Only the admin: add / deactivate people, change a role, set the HOURLY
   RATE of each employee, change permissions.


==============================================================================
 4. GETTING STARTED: SIGNUP, TRIAL, LOGIN, TEAM
==============================================================================

 4.1 SIGNUP (public page /register)
   A company registers itself. The app creates, in one step:
     - the company,
     - a trial subscription (14 days) on the plan marked "default",
     - the first admin user.
   The platform admin can also create a company by hand (sales-assisted).
   Then the owner gets an invitation email and chooses his own password. The
   platform admin never chooses anybody's password.

   Business condition: at least one plan must exist and be marked "default"
   before anybody can sign up. Otherwise signup fails (setup error).

 4.2 EMAIL VERIFICATION AND PASSWORD RESET
   - 6-digit code by email to verify the company email.
   - Forgot password: 2 steps. First enter the code (checked but not used),
     then choose the new password. Wrong code: 5 tries maximum.

 4.3 THE 4 WAYS TO LOG IN
   1. Company user: email + password.
   2. Platform admin: email + password + 2FA code.
   3. Worker on phone: email OR an 8-digit "login code" + a 4-6 digit PIN.
      The PIN locks after 5 wrong tries.
   4. Client portal: a private link (no login).

   Sessions: an access token for 15 minutes, a refresh token for 7 days.
   People can log out of one device or of all devices. Deactivating an
   employee cuts all his sessions at once. A user can see his active
   sessions in his account page.

 4.4 ADDING EMPLOYEES (admin only)
   Three ways:
     a) INVITATION: the admin enters name, email, role. The person gets a
        link (valid 7 days) and chooses a password.
     b) DIRECT with a temporary password (any role): the person must change
        the password at the first login.
     c) WORKER WITHOUT EMAIL: the admin gives name + PIN. The app creates an
        8-digit login code. The worker uses it on the phone only. No
        dashboard, no "forgot password" (the admin sets a new PIN).

   Fields of an employee: name, email, phone, role, hourly rate, active/not.
   Deactivated people cannot log in, but ALL their history stays.

 4.5 THE TRIAL AND WHAT HAPPENS AT THE END
   Day 1  : trial starts (14 days).
   Day 11 : reminder "your trial ends in 3 days, add a payment method".
   Day 14 : trial over. Status "past_due" + 7 days of grace. The app still
            works. A banner on every page says how many days are left.
   Day 21 : still no payment -> company BLOCKED. People can still log in and
            see the billing page to pay, but nothing else works. NO DATA IS
            EVER DELETED.
   Payment at any time: everything reopens at once.

 5. COMPANY PROFILE AND SETTINGS
==============================================================================

 The company edits its own profile (only roles with "settings" edit right;
 by default the admin).

 Editable: company name, legal name, VAT number, registration number, phone,
 address, IBAN, BIC, default VAT rate (default 21%), default payment days
 (default 30), language (fr / en / nl), time zone, the time of the daily
 hours reminder (default 18:00), logo.

 NOT editable by the company: email and currency (EUR), status.

 Why it matters for the business:
   - VAT number, address, IBAN, BIC and logo are printed on quotes and
     invoices.
   - "Default payment days" fills the due date of each new invoice.
   - "Default VAT rate" pre-fills each new document line.
   - The company language decides the language of PDFs and automatic emails.
     French is the fallback.

 Settings pages in the app: Company, Roles & permissions, Billing, Email
 (mailboxes), Support (tickets), Feedback.


==============================================================================
 6. CLIENTS
==============================================================================

 A client card = one person or company that pays.

 Fields: type (individual / professional / property manager), name, contact
 name, EMAIL (required), PHONE (required, format checked), second phone,
 VAT number (required only for "professional"), address, internal note,
 active/archived.

 Rules
   - Email is required because everything client-facing goes by email (quote,
     invoice, reminders, portal link).
   - Email is unique PER COMPANY: one card per person, so the whole history
     (all projects over the years) stays on one card.
   - Clients are never deleted, only archived.
   - The internal note is never shown in the portal.
   - A client can have extra email addresses (used by the email feature).

 Client page shows: info, linked projects, quotes, invoices.
 Access: sales and manager/admin can edit; accountant only reads.


==============================================================================
 7. CATALOGUE: SERVICES, MATERIALS, CATEGORIES, COST TYPES, SUPPLIERS
==============================================================================

 The catalogue is "what we SELL" and "what we STOCK".

 7.1 SERVICES - what we sell
   One row per sellable service with a selling price excl. VAT, a unit
   (m2, hour, piece...), a category and an optional default VAT rate (for
   example labour at 6%).
   Example: "Painting per m2", EUR 12.00 / m2.

 7.2 CATEGORIES
   A real list (Painting, Tiling, Plumbing...). No free text, so no typos
   and easy reports. The platform provides default categories; each company
   can add its own.

 7.3 MATERIALS - what we stock
   One row per material: description, unit, current PURCHASE price, minimum
   stock level (alert threshold). There is NO quantity stored on a material:
   quantity always comes from the stock ledger (section 14).
   Two materials of one company cannot have the same description.

 7.4 THE RECIPE (service <-> materials)
   For each service: which materials it consumes per 1 unit sold.
   Example: 1 m2 Painting = 0.15 L paint + 0.05 roll of tape + 0.02 kg filler.
   The recipe is used to: (a) reserve stock when a quote is accepted,
   (b) pre-fill the material usage on a site report.
   The recipe is THEORY. Real use on site is always edited by a person.

 7.5 COST TYPES
   Three defaults: material, subcontractor, labor. The company admin can add
   his own (insurance, machine rental...). The margin screen groups costs by
   cost type, so a new type appears by itself.

 7.6 SUPPLIERS
   Address book of material suppliers (name, email, phone, address, VAT
   number). Archived, never deleted.

 7.7 PICKING A SERVICE ON A QUOTE/INVOICE LINE
   The line description accepts free text OR a catalogue service. Picking a
   service COPIES its description, unit, price and VAT onto the line. A later
   catalogue price change never changes an old quote. A catalogue line
   reserves stock when accepted; a free-text line reserves nothing.


==============================================================================
 8. PROJECTS (THE CENTER OF EVERYTHING)
==============================================================================

 A project = one job for one client at one site address.

 Fields: client, name ("Dubois - bathroom"), description, status, SITE
 address (often different from the client's), planned start/end, real end
 date, project manager.

 4 STATUSES
   prospect ....... created, not confirmed yet.
   in_progress .... quote accepted, work is active.
   completed ...... work finished.
   cancelled ...... cancelled. FINAL.

 ALLOWED MOVES (everything else is refused)
   prospect    -> in_progress, cancelled
   in_progress -> completed, cancelled
   completed   -> in_progress   (ADMIN only, and it voids the closing snapshot)
   cancelled   -> nothing. A client who comes back gets a NEW project.

 AUTOMATIC EFFECTS
   - Quote accepted      -> project becomes in_progress.
   - Quote refused       -> project stays prospect.
   - Project cancelled   -> unused stock reservations are released, and an
                            alert reminds the manager to invoice the client
                            for the work already done.
   - Completed/cancelled -> the final financial numbers are frozen in a
                            "closing snapshot" (see section 17).
   - Every status change is written in a history.

 WHAT A PROJECT DOES NOT STORE (on purpose)
   - Budget       -> sum of accepted quotes.
   - Progress %   -> the newest site report.
   - Real cost and margin -> computed live.
   This means they can never be out of date.

 CLOSING IS NEVER BLOCKED BY MONEY
   "Completed" means "work finished", not "fully paid". Clients often pay
   weeks later. A cost or invoice that arrives AFTER closing is still saved
   and shows in the live margin, but does not change the frozen snapshot. To
   refresh the official number, the admin reopens and closes again.

 DELETE
   A project can be really deleted ONLY while it is "prospect" (nothing
   committed yet). Its files are deleted with it.

 PROJECT SUB-PAGES (in the app)
   Overview, Quotes, Invoices, Purchases, Subcontracting, Tasks, Time,
   Reports, Materials, Margin, Media (files), Chat, Emails, Client portal,
   History.

 9. QUOTES
==============================================================================

 A quote is the PRICE OFFER inside a project. It is 100% owned by the
 company; the client can only view, accept or refuse it.

 WHO BUILDS IT
   A company employee (owner, sales, manager). The person who visits the site
   builds the quote directly in the app, from a computer or a phone.

 CONTENT
   Client (required), project (required), number (QUO-2026-0001), issue date,
   "valid until" date, lines, note/terms, 3 totals (excl. VAT, VAT, incl. VAT).
   Each LINE: description, unit, quantity, unit price excl. VAT, VAT rate
   (6 or 21), optional link to a catalogue service, position.

 4 STATUSES AND MOVES
   draft    -> sent
   sent     -> draft (to fix a price), accepted, refused
   accepted -> nothing (final)
   refused  -> nothing (final; the client gets a NEW quote if he changes mind)

 RULES
   - A quote with no client or no lines cannot be accepted (no more "NaN"
     totals).
   - While DRAFT the totals recalculate at every line change. Once SENT the
     lines and totals are FROZEN. A sent paper never changes.
   - When sent, a PDF copy is saved and LOCKED forever (cannot be deleted).
     If the quote goes back to draft and is sent again, a NEW locked PDF is
     made.
   - "Valid until" is a HARD block: after that date the quote cannot be
     accepted. Prices may have changed. Staff re-sends it with a new date.
   - Who can accept: the client in the portal (Accept / Refuse buttons), or
     staff on behalf of a client who answers by phone. Both write the same
     data (date of acceptance is always real).
   - An accepted quote cannot be "undone". If the deal falls apart, cancel
     the PROJECT.
   - A quote can only be accepted while the project is prospect or
     in_progress.

 WHEN A QUOTE IS ACCEPTED (automatic)
   1. Date of acceptance saved.
   2. Project becomes in_progress.
   3. Stock is RESERVED for the materials the quote will need (section 14).
      This is a SOFT alert: the quote can be accepted even when stock is low;
      an alert says "not enough stock, order more".

 EXTRA WORK
   Extra work in the middle of a project = a SECOND quote, not an edit of the
   first. The budget grows automatically (10,000 + 1,500 = 11,500).

 The quote PDF is in the company language (French by default).


==============================================================================
 10. CLIENT PORTAL (WHAT THE CLIENT SEES)
==============================================================================

 A read-only web page for ONE project. No login, no account: the link itself
 is the key. Example: https://app.../portal/<long random token>

 CREATION
   - MANUAL only: the company clicks "Generate link" on the project page.
     Never automatic (otherwise a chat would open for every prospect that is
     never won).
   - One active link per project. Generating a new link kills the old one.
   - Valid 90 DAYS, then it dies by itself. If the project lasts longer, the
     company generates a new link.
   - The company can REVOKE a link at any time (wrong email, cancelled
     project). It stops working at once.
   - Expired or revoked link shows: "This link has expired. Please contact
     your company."
   - The token is stored hashed; the raw token exists only in the link.

 WHAT THE CLIENT SEES
   - Quotes: only "sent" and "accepted" (never draft, never refused).
   - Progress %: from the latest site report.
   - Invoices: sent, partially paid, paid (never draft or cancelled). A late
     invoice shows a red "late" label.
   - Documents shared with him.
   - Messages: he can write to the company and attach files (images + PDF,
     10 MB each, max 10 files per message).
   - He can ACCEPT or REFUSE a "sent" quote. This is the ONLY thing he can
     change.

 WHAT THE CLIENT NEVER SEES
   Margin, costs, subcontractors, supplier bills, employee names and hours,
   stock data, internal notes.

 TRACKING
   Every visit and download is logged ("opened 3 times, downloaded the invoice
   once"), so the company knows if the client really looked at the quote.

 CHAT IN THE PORTAL
   No live connection for the client (v1): the page refreshes about every 20
   seconds. The client also gets an email when the company replies. The
   company staff get the client's messages live.


==============================================================================
 11. PLANNING (TASKS AND GANTT)
==============================================================================

 The schedule of a project: a list of TASKS shown on a timeline (Gantt).

 TASK: project, title, type (work / meeting), start date, end date (cannot be
 before start), status (planned -> in_progress -> completed), one or more
 ASSIGNED EMPLOYEES.

 KEY RULES
   - Assigned people must be REAL employee accounts (never a typed name).
     Reason: the same person later logs his hours on that task.
   - A task with 5 workers is ONE task (one bar on the Gantt), 5 assignees.
   - A task needs at least one assignee before it can leave "planned".
   - One employee can work on several projects.
 Alerts: new task assigned, task starting tomorrow, task status changed.

 Example: Week 1 = demolition + plumbing rough-in, Week 2 = tiling, Week 3 =
 painting and finishing.


==============================================================================
 12. TIME ENTRIES (HOURS)
==============================================================================

 One row = one employee, one day, one project (optionally one task).

 Fields: project, employee, task (optional), date, hours (0-24), HOURLY RATE
 (frozen at that moment), comment ("finished tiling the floor").

 WHY THE RATE IS FROZEN
   The boss can change an employee's rate any time. Old entries keep their old
   rate, so past margins never move.

 WHO CAN LOG WHAT
   - The worker logs HIS OWN hours only, on projects where he is assigned to
     at least one task (project-level check; the task itself is optional).
   - A team leader can log his own and his team's hours.
   - Manager/admin can edit or delete any entry at any time (old value kept in
     the audit log).
   - The employee can edit his own entry only the SAME day.

 DAILY LIMIT (all projects together, per employee per day)
   up to 12 h ..... saved normally.
   over 12 h ...... saved + an "abnormal hours" alert to the manager.
   over 24 h ...... REFUSED (impossible).

 MANAGER VIEW
   Just the hours, plainly: who, which project, which date, how many hours.
   Filter by employee / project / date. No "activity score".

 ALERTS
   - Employee: end-of-day reminder (default 18:00) if no hours today.
   - Manager: "missing timesheet" (a task is in progress today but no hours);
     "abnormal hours"; "stalled project" (in_progress and no hours for 5 days).


==============================================================================
 13. SITE REPORTS
==============================================================================

 The daily record of what happened on site. One report per project per day.

 Content: date, progress % (0-100), weather, note, PHOTOS, and the MATERIALS
 used that day.

 WHO: admin, manager, site supervisor, team leader. NOT the worker, sales or
 accountant.

 IT FEEDS 3 THINGS
   1. The client portal (progress % of the latest report).
   2. Stock (the "Declare material used" action writes the consumption).
   3. Photos (attached to the report).

 DECLARING MATERIAL USED (the only action that moves stock out)
   1. Open today's report. 2. Click "Declare material used".
   3. Pick the service done (e.g. Painting, 20 m2).
   4. The recipe pre-fills quantities (3 L paint...).
   5. The person EDITS them to the real numbers (3.5 L).
   6. Save: one consumption line per material, at the material's current
      price (frozen).
   Pre-fill gives speed; editing gives truth. Never automatic.

 ALERTS: "missing report" (in_progress, no report for 3 days), "progress
 stalled" (same % for 7 days). Both go to the manager and the admin.


==============================================================================
 14. STOCK (MATERIALS)
==============================================================================

 STOCK IS COMPANY-WIDE
   One shared pool for all projects, not one stock per project. If two
   projects both need tiles, they draw from the same pile. That is where
   shortages happen.

 THE LEDGER (stock_movements)
   Quantity is NEVER stored as a number you edit. It is the SUM of a ledger of
   movements. 3 kinds:
     purchase ..... we received goods (+). Belongs to no project.
     consumption .. used on a project (-). Must name the project. Comes only
                    from a site report.
     adjustment ... a correction (+ or -). Needs a note. Can be linked to a
                    project.
   Each line freezes the unit price at that moment. The ledger is append-only:
   a mistake is fixed by a new adjustment, never by editing.

 STARTING STOCK
   When creating a material, an optional "Starting stock" creates ONE
   adjustment "Stock initial" at the material's price.

 TWO NUMBERS FOR EACH MATERIAL
   on_hand   = sum of the ledger.
   reserved  = what accepted quotes are holding.
   available = on_hand - reserved.

 RESERVATIONS (SOFT)
   When a quote is accepted, its catalogue lines walk through the recipes and
   reserve materials. Example: 20 m2 painting -> reserve 3 L paint, 1 roll
   tape, 0.4 kg filler.
   - Not enough stock? The quote is still accepted, but an alert fires.
   - New stock arrives? Reservations fill themselves and the alert clears.
   - Consumption lowers the remaining reservation (the original is kept).
   - Project cancelled: reserved-but-unused stock is released; what was
     already consumed stays consumed.
   - A second accepted quote adds to the existing reservation.
   - Free-text lines reserve nothing (on purpose).

 ALERTS: stock at or below minimum; reservation cannot be covered.

 CORRECTING A WRONG CONSUMPTION
   The manager adds an adjustment linked to the SAME project. +5 gives 5 units
   back to the stock AND lowers that project's material cost by 5 x the
   current price.

 Who: admin/manager full; site supervisor, team leader, accountant view. The
 worker never touches stock.


==============================================================================
 15. SUBCONTRACTING
==============================================================================

 For work done by an outside person or company (e.g. an external plumber).

 3 LEVELS
   Subcontractor (business card) -> Contract (one job) -> Purchase invoices
   (the bills).

   a) SUBCONTRACTOR: name, trade, email, phone (validated), optional hourly
      rate. Created once, reused for every future job.
   b) CONTRACT: subcontractor + ONE project, scope, agreed price, start/end
      dates, status (in_progress / completed / cancelled).
   c) BILLS: the subcontractor's invoices (see section 16). One contract can
      produce several bills (stage payments).

 PRIVACY
   Contracts and subcontractors are INTERNAL. The client never sees the
   subcontractor's name or price. The client sees only what the company
   charges (EUR 2,900); the plumber's EUR 2,500 stays hidden. The
   difference (EUR 400) is the company's margin on that work.

 Subcontractors can be edited later (phone validated). Plan limit counts only
 ACTIVE subcontractors.


==============================================================================
 16. PURCHASE INVOICES (MONEY OUT)
==============================================================================

 A bill the company RECEIVES. Somebody else wrote it. The app only records it
 so the company knows what it owes and counts the cost.

 2 TYPES (who sent it)
   subcontractor ... needs a contract and a project.
   supplier ........ needs a supplier. Project optional (see below).

 Fields: our number (PUR-2026-0007), their number, amount excl. VAT, VAT
 rate, issue date, due date, status (to_pay / paid), payment reference, paid
 date, PDF of their invoice (PDF only), cost type.

 THE IMPORTANT RULE: COST TYPE vs TYPE
   "Type" = WHO sent the paper. "Cost type" = WHAT KIND of cost.
   - Subcontractor bill (cost type subcontractor): always has a project and
     COUNTS in that project's margin.
   - Material bill (cost type material): NEVER has a project and does NOT
     count in the margin. Reason: the tiles are counted when they LEAVE the
     stock. Counting the bill too would count them TWICE.

 BUYING MATERIAL = TWO RECORDS
   - Stock "purchase" line: how many units do we now have?
   - Purchase invoice: how much do we owe, to whom, by when?
   They can exist alone (a correction needs no bill; transport needs no stock
   line). When both exist they are linked.

 UNPAID BILLS STILL COUNT
   A bill counts in the margin from the moment it is entered, whether paid or
   not. A cost you owe is a cost. Paying it later only says money left the
   bank.

 NO INSTALMENTS FOR MONEY OUT (v1)
   Status is only to_pay / paid. For stage payments, the contract simply
   produces several bills, each paid in full.

 The two invoice types NEVER feed each other. The client's invoice is based on
 the quote. The supplier's bill is only for internal cost tracking. (Re-billing
 a cost to the client with a mark-up is a possible future feature; it does not
 exist now.)

 ALERT: purchase bill due soon.


==============================================================================
 17. MARGIN AND PROFITABILITY
==============================================================================

 The margin is the money the company KEEPS on a project.

   margin = budget - material cost - labour cost - other bills
   margin % = margin / budget x 100

 WHERE EACH PART COMES FROM
   Budget ........ sum of ACCEPTED quotes (excl. VAT). Never typed.
   Material cost . stock ledger: quantity x frozen price (consumptions, plus
                   adjustments linked to the project).
   Labour cost ... hours x frozen hourly rate.
   Other bills ... purchase invoices of the project, grouped by cost type,
                   WITHOUT the "material" type.
   Three doors, never four. Each cost enters ONE time.

 LIVE WHILE THE PROJECT IS ACTIVE
   Computed fresh every time (a database view). Never saved as a fixed number.

 FROZEN WHEN THE PROJECT CLOSES
   When a project becomes completed OR cancelled, a "closing snapshot" saves
   the final budget, total cost and margin (plus one line per cost type).
   - Cancelled projects get a snapshot too (to freeze the cost of a dead job).
   - Reopening a completed project VOIDS the old snapshot (never deletes it).

 EARLY WARNING
   80% of budget reached -> WARNING   (to admin + manager, in-app + email)
   95% of budget reached -> CRITICAL  (same people)
   Each level fires ONCE per project (no spam at every save). If an extra
   accepted quote raises the budget and the cost falls back under the limit,
   the memory is reset and the alerts can fire again.

 EXAMPLE (Dubois)
   Budget 10,000. Materials 400 + plumber 2,500 + hours 580 = cost 3,480.
   Margin = 6,520 = 65.2%. Healthy.
   Plumber sends a second bill of 1,800 and the team works more hours:
   cost climbs to 8,050 = 80.5% of the budget -> WARNING alert, even though
   the work is not finished. That is the point of the feature.

 Who: admin, manager, accountant (full). Others no access.


==============================================================================
 18. CLIENT INVOICES AND PAYMENTS (MONEY IN)
==============================================================================

 An invoice is what the client owes the company. It is created in the app by
 staff and sent to the client.

 CONTENT
   Client, project (both required), optional quote link, number (INV-2026-0001),
   issue date, DUE DATE (required), lines (with VAT per line), 3 totals,
   note/payment terms, reminder counter, date of last reminder.

 SPLITTING
   One project can have many invoices (30% deposit, 40% progress, 30% final -
   the company decides). The system only WARNS if the invoices do not add up to
   the accepted quotes. It does not block.

 5 STATUSES (only about how much is paid)
   draft ........... being prepared.
   sent ............ sent to the client.
   partially_paid .. some money received.
   paid ............ fully paid (also if overpaid).
   cancelled ....... dropped; keeps its number and leaves a gap.

 NO "OVERDUE" STATUS
   Late is CALCULATED every time: due date is past AND a balance remains. A
   sent or partially paid invoice can both be late. The screen shows the status
   plus a red "late" label. A daily job only SENDS reminders; it changes no data.

 DUE DATE IS REQUIRED
   Otherwise an invoice could never be late and never be chased. It is
   pre-filled from "default payment days" (30) and can be changed per invoice.

 PAYMENTS (a ledger, never a running total)
   Each payment: amount, method (transfer, cheque, cash, stripe), reference,
   note, date, who recorded it.
   - Staff record payments by hand after seeing the bank transfer, cheque or
     cash. There is no automatic bank sync in v1.
   - Payments are NEVER edited or deleted.
   - A mistake is fixed with a CORRECTION row (negative amount, with a
     required note). Total paid can never go below 0.
   - Status is recomputed from the ledger after each row, so a correction can
     reopen "paid" into "partially_paid".
   - balance due = invoice total incl. VAT - total paid.

 RULES
   - Draft: lines and totals recalculate. Sent: everything frozen. A PDF copy
     is saved and locked.
   - An invoice cannot be "sent" with no client.
   - A cancelled invoice keeps its number.

 LATE INVOICES AND REMINDERS
   A daily job finds late invoices and alerts the admin and manager.
   The client receives a late-payment reminder by email.

 EXAMPLE
   Invoice 1: EUR 3,000 + 21% VAT = 3,630. Client pays 3,630 -> paid.
   Invoice 2: 4,840. Client pays 2,420 -> partially paid, balance 2,420.
   Due date passes -> still "partially paid" + red "late" + alert.
   Invoice 3: draft, still being prepared.

 Who: admin and accountant full; sales view.


==============================================================================
 19. DOCUMENT NUMBERS, VAT AND PDFs
==============================================================================

 NUMBERS
   Quote QUO-2026-0001 / Invoice INV-2026-0042 / Purchase PUR-2026-0007.
   - 4 digits, resets each January, unique per company (two companies can
     both have INV-2026-0001).
   - Given at creation (draft), never changes.
   - NEVER reused. A cancelled document leaves a gap; accountants expect that.
   - Two people clicking "create" at the same second get two different
     numbers (the counter is locked).

 VAT (TVA)
   - The VAT rate is ON EACH LINE, not on the document. In Belgium renovation
     work is often 6% while supplies are 21%, on the same document.
   - The company sets the default (21%), staff can change it per line; a
     service can have its own default rate (labour 6%).
   - Totals: lines are grouped by rate, VAT is rounded ONCE per rate group,
     then added. The printed breakdown by rate is read from the lines.
   - Margins are always EXCL. VAT. Cash views (invoiced, collected, open) are
     INCL. VAT.

 PDFs
   Quotes and invoices are generated as PDF in the company language, with
   logo, address, VAT number, IBAN/BIC (invoices), due date and note. When
   sent, the PDF is locked forever.

 22. EMAIL WITH CLIENTS
==============================================================================
 (Rules decided on 2026-10-18/19. Plan step 25. Check section 36 for the
 build status.)

 GOAL: company staff talk to clients by EMAIL from inside ChantierOS. Emails
 and portal messages appear TOGETHER in one chat per client (on our side only).
 We NEVER read a mailbox. We only receive what is sent or forwarded to our
 address, and we send only what the user writes and sends.

 3 KINDS OF MAILBOX
   1. COMPANY ADDRESS (default): company-x@mail.<our domain>. Free, every plan.
      SHARED inbox: everyone whose role has the "email" permission reads it.
   2. PERSONAL GMAIL: connected by its owner. Mail comes in by a Gmail forward;

   3. OWN DOMAIN (contact@companyx.be): idea kept open, NOT built.
   Outlook/Microsoft 365: later.

 PERMISSION "email": default admin, manager, sales full. Never the worker.
 The admin decides which roles can use it and connect a Gmail.

 ONE CHAT PER CLIENT, WITH TOPICS
   Chat "Eya Jarraya" contains topics: a portal chat, an email thread "Quote
   bathroom", another thread "Invoice question"... Each TOPIC has a project
   tag (or "no project"). So one client can talk about several projects.

 HOW THE PROJECT TAG IS CHOSEN
   1. Reply in an existing topic -> same tag.
   2. Email started from a project page -> that project.
   3. Portal message -> the project of the portal link.
   4. New email from the client: 0 open projects = no tag; 1 open project =
      suggested tag; 2+ open projects = no tag and an alert asks to link it.
   5. The tag can always be changed by the right users.

 WHO IS THE CLIENT
   The client whose email matches (a client can have several addresses). A
   reply from another address lands in the right chat with a warning "new
   address - add it?" (never added automatically). An unknown sender goes to
   "Unknown contact" with a "Create client" button.

 REPLIES GO TO THE ADDRESS THE CLIENT USED: Gmail -> Gmail, work address ->
 work address, portal -> portal.

 HOW A CLIENT'S REPLY FINDS ITS TOPIC
   Company address: a hidden reply address with a token.
   Personal Gmail: every subject ends with a topic code like [#K7Q2M9].
   Otherwise the email starts a new topic. No guessing by subject. A wrongly
   placed email can be MOVED to another topic.

 WHAT IS SAVED
   The original email (HTML + text, subject, from/to/cc), the attachments (as
   files, counting in storage). Kept forever (no auto delete). Auto-replies
   (holiday messages) are NOT saved.
   GDPR: the admin can delete a client's whole email chat on request.

 BILLING: each plan has "email_mailboxes" (some Gmail mailboxes included,
 extra ones billed at renewal). Connecting is never blocked; there is no buy
 button.

 SECURITY: Google tokens are stored encrypted. If a user is deactivated his
 Gmail is disconnected and his private chats stay locked. If Google access is
 lost, sending STOPS with a clear message (it is never sent from another
 address).

 ALERTS: new_email (in-app only if the person is online, in-app + email if
 not), mailbox disconnected, Gmail forward confirmation.

 AI on emails: ON HOLD, not planned for now.

 30. SUBSCRIPTION AND BILLING (WHAT THE COMPANY PAYS CHANTIEROS)
==============================================================================

 PLANS (set by the platform super-admin)
   A plan = a monthly base price + a value for each of 6 dimensions (and a
   7th, mailboxes, for Gmail):
     max_workers ........ active worker accounts (phone only)
     max_managers ....... active other employees (dashboard)
     max_clients ........ active client cards
     max_subcontractors . active subcontractor cards
     storage_gb ......... total file size
     retention_days ..... how long history is kept (not billed)
     email_mailboxes .... Gmail mailboxes
   PROJECTS ARE UNLIMITED. Never counted, never billed.

 PRICING MODEL: USAGE-BASED, NO BLOCKING
   bill = base price + sum of (actual use - included) x extra price per unit
   - Counted at each renewal. "Active" only: a company that deactivates an
     employee pays less next month.
   - Going over NEVER blocks anything. The 6th employee on a 5-employee plan is
     allowed and billed as an extra.
   - A rate of 0 means unlimited for free.
   - No refund or proration in the middle of a month.
   - Storage is the one exception: a DOWNGRADE of storage is refused while the
     company still holds more data than the new allowance.

 PLANS ARE VERSIONED
   A plan in use is never edited. A change creates a NEW version; existing
   companies stay on the old one until the platform admin moves them. A plan
   change for a company starts at the next renewal. The default plan cannot be
   deactivated. A public pricing page lists the active plans.

 PAYING (Stripe)
   The admin pays on a Stripe Checkout page. Stripe charges the card every
   month; ChantierOS adds the overage as an invoice item. Paying during the
   trial saves the card and charges on the last trial day. Card change and
   Stripe invoices are in the Stripe customer portal.
   Cancel: allowed; works until the end of the paid month; no refund; then
   blocked (data kept). "Keep my subscription" undoes a cancel before the end.
   A blocked company that pays starts a new month on the payment day.
   VAT on the subscription: not now.

 FAILED PAYMENT: the same as the trial end - 7 days of grace then block. A
 second failure does not extend the date. A failure never reopens a blocked
 company.

 BILLING HISTORY: one block per finished period: usage vs included, plan
 price + overage = total paid. Amounts are read from Stripe, never recomputed.
 "Payment received" email after each paid invoice (not for 0 EUR trial).

 33. FULL STORY: ONE PROJECT FROM START TO END
==============================================================================

 1. Mr. Dubois calls. The office creates a CLIENT card (individual, email,
    phone).
 2. A PROJECT "Bathroom - Dubois" is created. Status: prospect.
 3. A manager visits the site and builds a QUOTE: demolition 800, plumbing
    2,500, tiling 3,200, painting 900, electricity 600 = 8,000 excl. VAT +
    VAT. The quote is SENT (PDF locked, email to the client).
 4. The office clicks "Generate link". Mr. Dubois opens the PORTAL (valid 90
    days), sees the quote and clicks ACCEPT.
 5. Automatic: quote accepted, project -> in_progress, stock reserved
    (tiles, paint...), alert if stock is not enough.
 6. PLANNING: Week 1 demolition + plumbing, Week 2 tiling, Week 3 painting.
    Each task has real employees assigned. Workers get alerts.
 7. SUBCONTRACTING: an external plumber (subcontractor card), a contract of
    2,500 for this project.
 8. ON SITE: workers log HOURS on their phones. The supervisor posts a SITE
    REPORT (progress %, photos) and declares MATERIAL used (recipe pre-fills,
    he corrects). Stock goes down; reservations go down.
 9. The plumber's BILL is entered (purchase invoice, 2,500, counts in the
    margin at once). The tile supplier's bill is entered separately (material
    type: no project, not counted twice).
10. MARGIN is live: 8,000 - materials - plumber - hours. At 80% of the budget
    a WARNING goes to the admin and manager, once.
11. After demolition and plumbing, a 50% deposit INVOICE (4,000 excl. VAT) is
    sent. Mr. Dubois pays by transfer. The accountant records the PAYMENT ->
    paid. At the end, the second invoice. If it is late: red label + reminders
    + alerts.
12. The portal always shows: the accepted quote, progress %, invoices with
    status. Mr. Dubois never calls to ask "where are we?". He can also write
    in the portal chat.
13. The supervisor closes the project: COMPLETED. The final margin is frozen
    in the closing snapshot.
14. Later: analytics shows revenue, conversion rate, best and worst margins.

 37. THINGS I NOTICED THAT ARE UNCLEAR OR CONTRADICTORY
==============================================================================

 These are business-level points to confirm. I did not change any file.

 1. "Overdue" status. The old doc "Scenarios and Explanation.md" and
    "Dashboard Pages.md" still list an "overdue" status. The newer note
    (client-invoices.md) says there is NO overdue status: late is calculated.
    The newer note wins.

 2. Project chat creation. chat-conversations.md says the project-client chat
    is auto-created when the portal link is generated. client-portal.md says
    the link is created manually only (to avoid chats for prospects).
    Both agree if the chat is created only when someone clicks "Generate
    link". Worth confirming.

 3. Old page documents still mention AI pages (Agents IA, HITL, Connectors,
    Tokens) and a "mobile chat (AI)". The notes say the AI layer is out of v1.
    "Dashboard Pages.md" is older than the notes.

 4. The progress tracker says "Frontend: planned, not started", but the
    frontend already has many pages (including notes, contacts, analytics).
    The tracker is out of date on that line.

 5. The email feature: client-email.md describes the company mailbox and
    personal Gmail as "Built" in one table, while state.md says step 25 is
    "planned, not built" with only test code. The status needs one clear
    answer.

 6. Worker "own" access on reports: reports are "own (view)" for the worker in
    the table, but site-reports.md says the worker cannot post reports. The
    meaning is: he can only read. A "Reports" page exists in /field.

 7. Quote status "sent -> draft". Allowed so staff can fix a price, but the
    client may already have seen the sent quote in the portal. The locked PDF
    stays forever. Think about how you want to explain this to a client.

 8. Portal link lasts 90 days only. A long project needs a new link by hand.
    There is no automatic reminder for this in the notes. Possible
    improvement.

 9. Invoices vs accepted quotes. The app only WARNS when invoices do not add
    up to the accepted quotes. It is a soft rule on purpose; staff are
    responsible for the final numbers.

10. Stock reservations: "soft" alert, never a hard block. This was a conscious
    choice (a company may buy materials after signing).

   - "Open questions" section of doc/Schema Proposal.md
   At the last read, the tracker's items were all decided; only the frontend
